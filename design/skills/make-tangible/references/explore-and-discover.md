# Explore and Discover

> **Status: accepted Make Tangible pattern reference.**
>
> Load this reference only after Make Tangible selects Explore and Discover as
> the artifact's primary pattern.

## The judgment it supports

Explore and Discover makes a purposeful possibility space perceptible when the
human does not yet have enough vocabulary, criteria, preference, or awareness
to specify or compare a final direction honestly.

It succeeds when the human discovers something useful: a criterion they did
not know to name, a preference they recognize in context, a blind spot, a
promising direction, a rejected assumption, or a better question. It does not
require exhaustive coverage, ranking, convergence, or one selected winner.

Useful situations include unfamiliar product or system territory, visual or
interaction taste that is easier to recognize than describe, early solution
shape, codebase-grounded brainstorming, and domains where the human does not
yet know what good can look like.

## Shape the possibility space

Start from the owning question and identify the consequential dimensions that
could change the human's understanding. Present a small set of coherent
directions that differ along those dimensions. Each direction should embody a
meaningful hypothesis rather than a collection of cosmetic variations.

Explain what each direction helps expose. Four differently colored versions of
the same dashboard do not reveal alternative information models. A dense
operations view, a narrative timeline, and a keyboard-first work queue may
surface different preferences about scanning, causality, and control.

Do not manufacture a fixed number of options. Stop adding directions when new
ones no longer reveal a materially different idea or criterion.

## Preserve consequential reality

Implementation may be simulated, but the surface must preserve the constraints
that matter to the declared exploration:

- representative scale and content shape;
- consequential normal, empty, loading, failure, and boundary states;
- accepted repository, product, or domain constraints;
- known limitations and incompatible assumptions; and
- the dimensions the human is being asked to react to.

Label invented data, speculative behavior, and omitted implementation
explicitly. A review queue shown with five tidy placeholder items cannot support
judgment about information density at five hundred items. A direction that
ignores a load-bearing constraint may still provoke an idea, but it cannot
support a conclusion about that constraint.

## Invite discovery rather than force selection

Let the human react to complete directions and individual ideas. Useful
reactions include:

- keep or reject;
- combine these parts;
- this surprised me;
- this reveals a new criterion;
- this assumption is wrong;
- explore this aspect further; and
- none of these frame the problem correctly.

Keep free-form reaction available. A single-choice control is insufficient when
the human wants one direction's navigation, another direction's recovery
model, and neither direction's density.

When the reaction cannot remain safely in conversation, export the selected,
rejected, mixed, surprising, and unresolved reactions against the exact judged
version. The export is discovery evidence for the caller to interpret, not a
final Requirement, Evolution, Issue, or Plan.

## Compose supporting patterns proportionately

Explore and Discover may use:

- Compare and Choose to make consequential contrasts legible;
- Elicit and Articulate to capture latent preference and newly named criteria;
- Experience or Simulate when behavior itself must be felt;
- Manipulate and Export when the reaction is compositional; and
- Understand and Navigate when unfamiliar structure blocks useful reaction.

Explore and Discover remains primary when the purpose is learning what matters,
not selecting among already-understood alternatives.

## Verify before presenting

Check that:

- the directions are genuinely distinct along the declared dimensions;
- representative constraints and states are present;
- factual, speculative, simulated, and placeholder content are distinguishable;
- consequential fidelity is comparable across directions, or visible
  differences narrow the supported reaction;
- order, polish, defaults, or interaction do not silently rank one direction;
- every control and state required for exploration works in the intended
  medium; and
- mixed and free-form reactions survive export accurately.

If the directions repeat the same idea, hide a load-bearing constraint, or
steer the human through unequal polish, revise the surface before asking for
judgment.

## Return to the caller

Return the exact artifact version together with:

- the vocabulary, criteria, preferences, blind spots, or questions discovered;
- promising and unhelpful directions, including the reason where known;
- mixed or qualified reactions;
- the constraints and fidelity limits under which the reactions were formed;
  and
- what remains unclear or deserves another bounded probe.

The calling loop decides what learning belongs in its governing record and
whether to continue exploring, investigate an unknown, converge on a
comparison, or recommend another path.

## Worked compositions

Thariq Shihipar's
[four design directions](https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/blob/main/unknowns/03-design-directions.html)

export. The
[churn brainstorm](https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/blob/main/unknowns/05-churn-brainstorm.html)

every idea a commitment. These are composition examples, not required layouts
or reusable templates.
