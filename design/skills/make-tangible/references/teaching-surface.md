# Teaching Surface

> **Status: accepted semantic source; target runtime projection: `references/teaching-surface.md`.**

> Load this reference only after Make Tangible selects Teaching Surface as the
> artifact's primary pattern.

## The judgment it supports

Teaching Surface constructs a faithful medium through which a caller can teach
one settled model or check one bounded learning objective. It is especially
useful when a diagram, annotated view, simulation, demonstration, or quiz
interface would reduce the human's need to reconstruct the subject mentally.

The caller—normally [Orient](../../orient/README.md)—supplies:

- the learning objective and human starting point;
- the settled model and authoritative evidence;
- the mechanism, boundaries, failures, and uncertainty that must remain
  visible;
- the conceptual teaching sequence or questions when any; and
- the intended lifetime and judgment the surface must support.

Make Tangible does not choose what the human ought to learn, own teaching
progression, interpret misconceptions, or decide that understanding has been
demonstrated. It owns how the supplied teaching need becomes perceptible and
reliably usable.

## Choose the smallest faithful form

Select form from the teaching need:

- a diagram for relationships, ownership, flow, or state;
- an annotated or navigable view for moving between overview and evidence;
- a walkthrough for representative behavior and failure paths;
- a simulation when state changes must be experienced;
- a demonstration when observed behavior anchors the model; or
- a quiz interface when questions, feedback, and retries need a constructed
  interaction.

Do not build a rich artifact when conversation, a short example, a simple text
diagram, or a small table can carry the model faithfully. Do not turn the
caller’s bounded objective into a general course or product tour.

## Preserve teaching fidelity

Trace every consequential claim to the supplied accepted meaning, current
source, or observed evidence. Label inference, simulation, omission, and
placeholder content. A polished teaching surface must not make uncertain or
mocked behavior appear established.

Keep terminology, diagrams, examples, transitions, and edge cases internally
consistent. Show the boundaries of the model and the conditions under which it
stops applying. Link deeper source evidence where useful without turning the
surface into a file listing.

If the supplied model is contradictory, incomplete, or unsupported, return the
conflict to the caller. Make Tangible does not repair the teaching objective,
invent governing truth, or make falsehood easier to understand.

## Build quiz mechanics without owning pedagogy

When the caller supplies conceptual questions, construct only the interaction
needed to present them, capture answers, show caller-supplied or evidence-backed
feedback, and permit useful retry.

- Do not add scores, grades, or a permanent learner profile by default.
- Do not replace applied questions with implementation trivia.
- Do not leak answers before the human commits to a judgment.
- Changed retry scenarios should test reasoning rather than memorization.
- Wrong-answer feedback must point to the relevant model or evidence.
- A quiz result never proves the implementation, approves meaning, grants
  authority, or accepts a result.

Orient or another pedagogical caller interprets the response and decides what
explanation follows. Make Tangible reports the exact interaction outcome and
any mechanical or fidelity limitation without declaring comprehension.

## Compose supporting patterns proportionately

Teaching Surface may use:

- Understand and Navigate for a navigable model;
- Experience or Simulate for mechanisms that must be felt;
- Inspect and Annotate for source-grounded explanation;
- Observe and Assess for measured behavior; and
- Demonstrate and Prove for claims tied to executable evidence.

It remains primary when the constructed artifact exists to support a supplied
learning objective. The caller retains orientation, discovery, specification,
review, or result ownership.

## Verify before presenting

Check that:

- the supplied learning objective and starting point remain visible;
- every consequential claim traces to current evidence or accepted meaning;
- the chosen medium materially reduces comprehension cost;
- representation and interaction behave through the intended medium;
- diagrams, examples, questions, feedback, and edge cases describe one model;
- navigation preserves the relationship between overview and evidence;
- quiz answer handling and retries work without leaking answers;
- accessibility and rendering fit the intended audience and medium;
- the artifact states its fidelity limits and intended lifetime; and
- export captures the exact surface version and reaction without hidden state.

## Return to the caller

Return:

- the exact artifact version and reliable reference when one exists;
- the form chosen and the supplied learning objective it serves;
- authoritative evidence and fidelity boundaries;
- the captured reaction or answers without pedagogical interpretation;
- interaction, rendering, or accessibility limitations;
- the declared lifetime and retention state; and
- contradictions or missing inputs that prevented faithful construction.

The caller decides what the reaction means, whether another explanation is
needed, and whether the owning judgment can continue.

## Worked compositions

Thariq Shihipar's
[interactive consistent-hashing explainer](https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/blob/main/15-research-concept-explainer.html)
uses manipulation to support mechanism intuition. The
[color-grading unknowns explainer](https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/blob/main/unknowns/02-color-grading-explainer.html)
builds vocabulary through live examples, while the
[change quiz](https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/blob/main/unknowns/11-change-quiz.html)
combines a source-grounded change explanation with applied questions and retry.
They demonstrate construction mechanics rather than assigning Make Tangible
ownership of the learning objective or its interpretation.
