# Interpreting References

> **Status: accepted semantic source; target runtime projection: `references/reference-interpretation.md`.**

> Load this reference when an existing implementation, product, document,
> image, behavior, or other rich source carries more useful meaning than a
> verbal description. Discover Requirements retains the inquiry, synthesis,
> governing truth, gate, and stopping decision.

## The question it helps answer

Reference interpretation asks what exact behavior, structure, quality, or
constraint the human values or rejects, why the source produces it, and which
later judgment that meaning should inform. A link alone is not interpreted
context, and copying the source's entire implementation is not interpretation.

## Inspect the highest-fidelity source

Prefer the form closest to the relevant truth:

- working source for structure and semantics;
- observable behavior for interaction and failure;
- authoritative documentation for a declared contract;
- an image or recording for visual or temporal qualities; and
- a paper, standard, or report for its supported claim.

Record the exact path, URL, version, revision, environment, or observed state
needed to interpret the source correctly. Make volatile conditions visible.

## Separate meaning from accident

Identify:

- the exact behavior or quality the human values or rejects;
- the mechanism and conditions that produce it;
- the transferable intent;
- incidental language, framework, styling, or historical structure;
- incompatible assumptions or constraints; and
- the Requirement or later judgment the reference may inform.

For example, a Rust retry library may be valued for its backoff and
cancellation semantics, while its type layout and crate structure are
incidental to a TypeScript client.

## State the source's authority

Classify the reference for the current claim:

- **authoritative** when it defines the governing contract;
- **evidentiary** when it supports a factual conclusion;
- **exemplary** when it demonstrates one successful realization; or
- **inspirational** when it expands the possibility space.

Authority is claim-specific. Official documentation can define an API contract
while remaining irrelevant to the human's preferred interaction. An admired
implementation can be exemplary without becoming a Requirement.

## Preserve human interpretation separately

Record what the human wants to preserve, reject, combine, or understand
further. Do not attribute that judgment to the source itself.

When the human reacts to a partial or simulated representation, preserve the
fidelity boundary. Approval of a screenshot's density does not establish the
behavior of an interaction it cannot demonstrate.

## Promote only supported meaning

A Reference informs governing truth through supported evidence and human
judgment. It does not become governing truth merely because it was supplied,
popular, authoritative in another context, or technically impressive.

Return the narrowest supported interpretation. If the desired property depends
on an unverified mechanism or incompatible condition, recommend Research,
Make Tangible, or Interview rather than silently generalizing it.

## Return to Discover Requirements

Return:

- the exact source and relevant version or conditions;
- its authority for the claim at hand;
- the valued, rejected, or disputed qualities;
- the mechanism and transferable intent;
- incidental or incompatible details;
- the human interpretation and fidelity limits; and
- the Requirement or later judgment it may inform.

Discover Requirements decides what becomes accepted truth and what remains a
Reference, evidence, reaction, or open uncertainty.
