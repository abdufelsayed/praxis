# Working with Praxis

Praxis keeps one active Inquiry around the current question. Lifecycle working
forms and supporting capabilities serve that Inquiry; they are not a process
the requester must traverse or name. The agent navigates while the requester
retains consequential meaning, transitions, acceptance, and effect authority.

## The visible control loop

For a bounded request, expect this sequence:

1. **Current position.** The agent states the Inquiry's question, position,
   accepted context, provisional context, and unresolved conditions.
2. **Bounded work.** Supporting capabilities investigate, compare, interview,
   make tangible, review, or verify without asking for routine permission
   inside the Inquiry's policy.
3. **Meaningful transition.** When the position, accepted meaning, gate
   consequence, or posture should change, the agent explains convergence,
   recommendation, allowed work, unauthorized work, and material alternatives;
   the requester chooses in ordinary language.
4. **Authority at the effect.** Mutation or an external action waits for your
   exact action, target, and effect when the current request does not
   already contain it. Approval never supplies that authority.
5. **Evidence return.** The capability reports what happened, what it means,
   what remains valid, limitations, unresolved questions, and the causal
   record affected by changed truth.
6. **Stop.** Acceptance, exhaustion, refusal, invalidated truth, or a safe
  blocker returns control to you. Adjacent work is not implied.

When the current message already settles the question, position, and bounded
authority, no routing ceremony is needed: Using Praxis establishes the Inquiry,
loads the smallest useful capability, and exits. It does not perform that
capability's work or remain active above it.

Recommendations are navigation, not permission requests. A supporting skill
answers the bounded question its caller gives it and returns; it does not take
the caller’s gate, route, authority, or stopping rule. Once a supporting
condition is present, the active owner invokes it without asking you to
remember an internal skill, consumes its bounded return, and only then
completes that step.

## Choosing a proportionate path

Clear, reversible work can stay Direct Work or enter bounded Delivery without
a Plan when target, exclusions, risk, authority, and proof are already settled.
A Plan is useful when the approach, recovery, integration, or proof deserves
judgment before implementation.
Issues, Projects, Evolutions, and Initiatives exist only when their distinct
meaning improves judgment or continuity. Evolve requires at least two
independently meaningful working forms; size alone never creates it.

The requester chooses a meaningful transition in ordinary language. Approval of a
Requirements bundle, Evolution Map, Issue, or Plan accepts only that named
proposal. Plan Approval is followed by a separate Implementation Authorization,
even when both acts appear in one message.

## Supporting capabilities

Research, Interview, Make Tangible, Review, Verify, Security, Delegate, and
other capabilities are bounded calls. Their returns remain evidence or a
scoped result for the owner. Orient is a teaching interaction you select;
Pair is a collaborative implementation posture you request and is never
inferred or recommended. Plannotator presents a selected native interaction,
but its approval is not implementation authority.

## From research to Requirements

Research improves visibility; it does not decide what the product should be.
For substantial work, Praxis automatically updates one Research Findings
record, when the Inquiry's persistence policy permits it, with source talking
points, observed behavior, findings and inferences, research learnings,
contradictions, limitations, provisional implications, and its named consumer.
The active discovery conversation then turns relevant evidence
into concrete product scenarios, recommendations, and judgments.

Before product judgment begins, Praxis presents a compact transition brief. It
states the current product meaning, material uncertainty, ready judgments,
their dependency order, and the recommended first topic. A provisional topic
map makes the remaining inquiry visible. The requester selects the transition.

The agent should lead with the real question, make it tangible with a small
example when useful, recommend a direction with reasons, and continue through
dependent questions in ordinary conversation. Independent grounded questions
may be answered together through Plannotator. A pause or correction stops the
current framing immediately.

At a coherent topic closeout, accepted meaning becomes small atomic
Requirements, genuine uncertainty redraws the Unknowns Map, and every relevant
research finding is either promoted through accepted meaning, retained for a
named later decision, kept as an unknown, rejected, or treated as incidental.
This harvest occurs through an interview with the requester. Governing records
follow their own persistence and gate rules; research persistence never
promotes a finding automatically. New record types require a distinct purpose,
consumer, lifecycle, and authority boundary.

## Redirection and learning

When evidence may change accepted meaning, Praxis opens a challenge without
rewriting that meaning. It identifies the earliest causal owner and presents a
small impact map. The map shows affected descendants, unaffected meaning,
suspended authority, and the judgment needed.

After the requester accepts a causal change, Praxis traces it forward through
dependent records. Only affected meaning is revised and re-gated. Local
correction remains valid while accepted meaning, targets, exclusions,
consequences, proof, and authority remain intact. Otherwise, affected mutation
stops at a safe point and needs fresh authority after reconciliation.

Feedback types remain distinct: preference is not proof, critique is not
acceptance, and a passing check is not authority. Preserve only learning a
future owner needs; source, tests, Git, and artifacts remain the evidence for
reconstructible implementation truth.

## Resumption and context

On resumption, reconstruct the active Inquiry from the latest direction,
relevant accepted records, current source/tests/Git, and any
temporary Delivery checkpoint that is genuinely needed. Load context
progressively. The presence of a file does not prove that an Evolution was
selected, a Plan was authorized, or a result was accepted.
