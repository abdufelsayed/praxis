# Data protection and privacy

Load when sensitive or regulated data moves through the target.

Classify the data and trace collection, derivation, transport, storage,
indexes, caches, analytics, logs, errors, exports, support tools, backups,
replicas, retention, deletion, and third parties. Identify actors and services
that can observe each representation and whether access is necessary for the
accepted behavior.

Check minimization, purpose boundaries, tenant isolation, field-level access,
redaction, encryption and key separation, safe defaults, test data, debug
surfaces, deletion propagation, and recovery media. Encryption at rest does
not protect copies in logs, over-broad APIs, or indefinitely retained backups.
Separate a demonstrated confidentiality, integrity, or lifecycle violation
from a general privacy improvement; keep missing policy explicit.

Sources: [OWASP User Privacy Protection](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/User_Privacy_Protection_Cheat_Sheet.html),
[OWASP Logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html),
[NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework).
