# Coordinate Multiple Projects

Load this method only when one effort spans two or more independently owned
projects. Project-local requirements, issues, plans, implementation, and
acceptance remain with their owners.

Add only the shared navigation needed to reason across them:

- the strategic objective and observable shared success condition;
- participating projects and owners;
- what each project contributes and needs;
- shared constraints and open strategic decisions;
- hard dependencies;
- useful sequencing for learning, compatibility, risk, or value; and
- coordination convenience that does not constrain either project.

Keep these relationships in the effort map and link to project-owned detail
instead of duplicating it. A shared objective does not flatten the projects into
one backlog or transfer their authority. If no consequential objective,
relationship, or decision remains shared, split the effort instead of
maintaining it for reporting convenience.

Recommend the next shared frontier decision. The requester selects it; each
project owner separately authorizes its local work.
