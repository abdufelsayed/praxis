# Evolve — Maintainer Contract

This is the acceptance contract for the shipping
[Evolve owner](SKILL.md). It is not runtime
context and the owner must not link to or depend on it.

## Purpose and boundary

Evolve owns one Project's progressive strategy: its Evolution Map, one
human-selected Evolution definition, the judgments on those exact records,
and delivered learning that materially changes progression.

Evolve does not own Requirements, Issue definition, Plans, implementation,
delivery, supporting evidence work, human preference interpretation, artifact
construction, or the next-route choice. It may call those owners for bounded
results but retains synthesis, records, gates, route, authority, acceptance,
and stopping.

Behavioral entry requires an explicit human choice of Evolve. Host discovery,
skill loading, a recommendation, or an unaccepted suggestion is not entry. A
declined or unselected suggestion stops without creating a Map.

## Required behavior

### Entry and governing context

1. Entry provenance is `human-selected`, `declined-suggestion`, or
   `not-selected`. Only `human-selected` may create or revise Evolve records.
2. An entered run binds one accepted Project and Requirements revision,
   outcome and boundary, current position, evidence and limits, dependencies,
   change pressure, relevant preferences, caller, and current authority.
3. Missing governing context blocks without invention. Changed needs,
   constraints, Requirements, or Project boundary return their exact conflict
   to Requirements Discovery and stop affected work.
4. Project size, feature labels, technical layers, milestones, releases, or an
   Issue count never prove that an Evolution route is useful.

### Evolution Map

5. A Map contains the smallest serious sequence of at least two coherent,
   integrated working advances. Every advance is tangible enough to exercise,
   review, prove, and learn from.
6. Every entry has a stable descriptive ID, independent sequence position,
   tangible advancement, deliberate limitations, position value, and an
   optional learning target tied to a named later Map judgment.
7. Reordering preserves stable IDs. Splitting, merging, or replacing meaning
   creates new IDs and supersedes the displaced identity.
8. The Map explains how the sequence advances accepted Requirements and makes
   material dependencies, omissions, pressure, and uncertainty visible. It
   does not become a Requirement ledger or pre-write Evolutions, Issues, or
   Plans.
9. Several serious sequences are compared by consequences and one is
   recommended. If only one coherent advance exists, Evolve returns
   `one-advance`, recommends direct Issue definition, and waits for the requester
   to choose the route; it never manufactures a second advance or silently
   returns to a parent owner.
10. If every sequence is artificial because governing Project truth is wrong,
    Evolve returns the changed boundary to Requirements Discovery.

### Supporting calls

11. Every supporting call has one exact trigger, complete bounded brief,
    expected result, and return destination. Evolve preserves the callee's
    exact outcome, evidence, limits, partial state, blocker, stop, and changed
    meaning, then applies its local consequence.
12. Research answers one factual sequencing premise. `answered` may be
    integrated; `unverified` and `conflicting` remain limits; `no-op` names the
    sufficient current context; `revised` rebinds the premise;
    `needs-human-judgment` returns to its named owner; `blocked` and `stopped`
    remain distinct.
13. Interview owns one human working-form, ordering, criterion, or preference
    judgment. `clarified` and bounded `no-op` may be integrated; `qualified`,
    `deferred`, and `conflicting` remain human boundaries; `needs-evidence`
    returns to its causal owner; `blocked` and `stopped` remain exact. Changed
    Requirements return to Requirements Discovery.
14. Orient may be suggested for one settled model, but is called only after
    the requester accepts that suggestion. `explained`, `understanding-observed`,
    `understanding-unconfirmed`, `misconception-unresolved`,
    `orientation-blocked`, `causal-return`, and `stopped` retain distinct
    meanings. Declining it creates no comprehension claim.
15. Make Tangible is called only when a faithful artifact materially improves
    judgment. `no-op`, `constructed`, `revised`, `blocked`, and `stopped`
    remain distinct. Its exact artifact version, reaction, supported
    conclusions, fidelity limits, and lifetime are preserved; reaction is not
    approval.
16. Design Solution is called only with its complete choice boundary,
    accepted truth, candidates, constraints, evidence bar, authority, and
    caller-retained controls. `recommended` is not `selected`; `qualified` and
    `no-choice` remain usable but distinct; `revised` returns to its causal
    owner; `refused` and `stopped` remain exact. A missing preflight returns to
    its causal owner.
17. Plannotator is a gate surface, not a gate owner. Conversation is sufficient
    for one visible, unambiguous judgment; Plannotator is used when annotation,
    partial judgment, target size, or native fidelity materially helps.

### Human judgments and selection

18. Every gate names an exact target and content revision. Explicit approval
    accepts only that revision. Denial stops without approval. Feedback may
    revise the target but never passes the gate. Dismissal, empty closure, and
    human stop make no judgment. Command, transport, malformed, recovery, or
    uncertain-outcome failure blocks without silent fallback.
19. A governing-content change increments `content_revision`, invalidates its
    prior approval, and returns the exact record to pending judgment. A
    projection-only approval update does not change content revision.
20. Map Approval accepts only progressive strategy. It grants no Evolution
    selection, specification, Issue, Plan, mutation, acceptance, or forward
    authority.
21. Evolution Selection is a separate explicit human act naming one stable ID
    in the current approved Map. Approval, recommendation, sequence position,
    "continue", and silence do not select.
22. Before creating an Evolution file, the selection must resolve to exactly
    one current stable ID. Missing, duplicate, stale, and position-only targets
    block with the Map revision and repair condition and create no file.
23. Definition Approval accepts only the selected Evolution's current complete
    high-level working form. It recommends Issue definition and stops; it does
    not create Issues, plan, deliver, implement, or select another Evolution.

### Definition, acceptance, and learning

24. The selected definition contains governing truth, tangible advancement,
    included high-level behavior, integration, deliberate limitations,
    applicable learning target, required proof, and current recommendation.
    It stays above detailed interfaces, cases, acceptance criteria, files,
    commands, execution order, and checks.
25. Evolution Acceptance is requested only with the actual integrated result,
    proved coverage and evidence scope, unproved claims, checks and limits,
    remaining uncertainty, exact claim, and excluded adjacent actions visible.
26. Acceptance closes only its named proved coverage. Rejection and feedback
    preserve partial state. Native failure blocks. Acceptance never selects or
    starts the next Evolution.
27. Working-form proof remains separate from learning evidence. Changed needs,
    constraints, or Project boundary return to Requirements Discovery;
    changed high-level Evolution meaning returns to its definition owner;
    detailed behavioral gaps return to Issues.
28. Only learning that changes the number, order, boundary, meaning, or
    progression of future Evolutions revises the Map. The smallest affected
    Map change returns approval to pending and emits no new selection.

### Persistence and stopping

29. The Map lives at `<project>/evolutions/map.md`. A selected definition
    lives at `<project>/evolutions/eN-<name>.md`.
30. Current records use the shared record envelope and only the current
    approval projection when applicable. Evolve persists no
    transcript, approval history, decision ledger, generic Result, or
    implementation chronology.
31. Every approval, acceptance, causal return, supporting blocker or stop, and
    Map revision is terminal for that turn. A valid explicit selection may
    enter definition within Evolve, but grants no adjacent owner work. Evolve
    never advances by momentum.

## Effects, authority, persistence, and lifetime

Evolve may inspect the accepted Project and Requirements, current source and
evidence, existing owner records, and supporting results needed to judge
progression. After explicit Evolve entry it may create or revise only the
current Project's Evolution Map and the one human-selected Evolution
definition. It may project the current approval or acceptance judgment and
integrate evidence pointers or delivered learning that a future progression
decision needs.

Evolve may not create or revise Requirements, Issues, Plans, implementation,
source, tests, commits, releases, deployments, external state, or another
Evolution. Entry permits Map work only; each Map Approval, Evolution
Selection, Definition Approval, and Evolution Acceptance remains a separate
human judgment on an exact revision. Supporting results grant no mutation,
selection, gate, acceptance, or continuation authority.

The Map and selected Evolution use Record Foundations and Formalization Records
only when their durable meaning must survive the conversation or a named future
owner needs it. Current gate projection and accepted evidence pointers are
durable; transcripts, gate histories, task ledgers, implementation chronology,
generic Results, and dependency receipts are not. Supersession and retirement
preserve stable identity and live consumer needs under the shared record
lifetime rules.

## Direct composition

Every direct call carries the callee's complete bounded brief, preserves its
exact native return, and leaves Project synthesis, Map and Evolution records,
gates, route, authority, acceptance, and stopping with Evolve.

| Target | Exact trigger and return use | Evolve retains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Research](../research/SPEC.md) | One factual sequencing premise can change the Map or selected high-level form. Preserve every native outcome; integrate only supported factual meaning and keep uncertainty visible. | Premise interpretation, Map synthesis, gate, and stop. |
| [Interview](../interview/SPEC.md) | One human-owned working-form, ordering, criterion, or preference judgment is unresolved. Preserve qualifications, deferral, conflict, and evidence needs exactly. | Product synthesis, Requirements return, gate, and stop. |
| [Orient](../orient/SPEC.md) | One settled model must be understood for judgment and the requester accepts the teaching suggestion. Preserve comprehension limits and causal returns. | Truth, proof, gate, and acceptance. |
| [Make Tangible](../make-tangible/SPEC.md) | A faithful artifact materially improves Map or definition judgment. Preserve artifact identity, reaction, fidelity, lifetime, and blocked/stopped state. | Meaning, decision, evidence interpretation, and artifact use. |
| [Design Solution](../design-solution/SPEC.md) | One consequential solution choice inside progression has a complete preflight. Preserve recommendation, selection, qualification, revision, refusal, and stop without upgrading them. | Integration into the Map or definition, route, and gate. |
| [Plannotator](../plannotator/SPEC.md) | Annotation, partial judgment, target size, or native fidelity makes a native Map, Definition, or Evolution Acceptance surface useful. Preserve exact target, decision, feedback, reference, and failure state; never fall back silently. | Record meaning, gate consequence, acceptance, and stop. |

The shared Learning and Return reference is loaded only when changed meaning
must return to its earliest causal owner. It is not a direct skill result and
does not add a local fallback route.

## Observable outcomes

Evolve returns one of:

```text
map-proposed
map-approved
map-revised
one-advance
selection-required
evolution-selected
evolution-specified
definition-approved
evolution-accepted
causal-return
blocked
stopped
```

Every result identifies the current Project, Map ID and revision when one
exists, selected Evolution ID and revision when one exists, the exact human
judgment or missing choice, decisive evidence and limits, changed meaning or
partial state, and revisit condition. A result grants no authority beyond its
named consequence.

## Conformance scenarios

An implementation conforms only if all of these remain true:

1. An unaccepted Evolve suggestion creates no `.praxis` record and returns
   `stopped` with honest entry provenance.
2. A Project with two coherent working advances produces stable IDs whose
   identities survive reordering.
3. A Project with only one coherent advance returns `one-advance`, recommends
   direct Issues, and awaits the requester's route choice.
4. A Project-boundary conflict returns to Requirements Discovery without
   locally changing Requirements.
5. Map approval without a named stable ID returns `selection-required` and
   creates no Evolution definition.
6. A sequence-position-only, stale, missing, or duplicate selection returns
   `blocked` before file creation.
7. A valid selected definition remains high level and Definition Approval
   returns `definition-approved`, recommends Issues, and stops.
8. Research `needs-human-judgment`, an Interview qualification, a declined
   Orient suggestion, a Make Tangible fidelity limit, and a supporting
   `blocked` result each retain their distinct meaning.
9. Feedback that changes a Map produces a new content revision with approval
   pending; feedback that does not approve passes no gate.
10. Accepted delivered learning that does not change progression leaves the
    Map approval intact; learning that does change progression produces the
    smallest `map-revised` result with approval pending.
11. Conversation and Plannotator apply the same approval semantics, while
    dismissal, empty closure, denial, feedback, human stop, and native failure
    remain behaviorally distinct.
12. Every terminal result stops before Issue definition, planning, mutation,
    implementation, or another Evolution.

## Host projection

The frontmatter name is `evolve`. Its description remains exactly:

> Evolve a Project through a sequence of working forms selected by the
> requester. Use after the requester explicitly chooses Evolve.

Host metadata keeps implicit invocation disabled. Discovery metadata may
advertise Evolve, but it cannot satisfy behavioral entry.
