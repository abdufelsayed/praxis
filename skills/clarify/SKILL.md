# Clarify

Interview the user when the missing answer is theirs to give. Facts the environment can answer are looked up, never asked: inspect source, docs, and history first, and bring only decisions to the user.

## Interview

Ask one question at a time. With each question, give your recommended answer and why, so the user can confirm with a word instead of composing from scratch. Prefer the question whose answer most changes the next action.

Keep the interview proportional: one question may settle a bug report; a fuzzy feature may need a short arc across purpose, constraints, scope, and risks. Stop interviewing when the next action is unambiguous.

## Record

Keep one-turn alignment in conversation. When answers govern work that outlives the session, route the outcome through the owning skill: accepted choices to `decision`, observable behavior to `spec`, executable work to `plan` or `issues`.
