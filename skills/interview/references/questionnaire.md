# Prepare a Questionnaire

Load this method only when the requester asks for a questionnaire for another
person to answer asynchronously or during a meeting.

Grill the send, not the subject. Establish the recipient's role, expertise,
relationship to the requester, and what they know that the requester does not.
Then establish the exact facts or decisions the requester must receive and what
those answers will enable. Do not ask the requester to supply the missing domain
knowledge on the recipient's behalf.

Write the artifact only when the requester asks for a durable questionnaire or
names a path. Use the smallest useful structure:

- title and purpose;
- sender, recipient, and how the answers will be used when useful;
- enough context for someone who was not in the conversation;
- how to answer, including timing or effort when known and permission for
  partial or uncertain answers;
- questions grouped by theme and ordered by consequence;
- one idea per question, with an answer stub directly beneath it;
- a short "why this matters" only when the question could be misread; and
- an open "Anything else?" prompt.

Every requested fact or decision must map to at least one question. Report the
path and uncovered gaps. Creating the questionnaire does not answer it, send it,
or authorize action from its future answers.
