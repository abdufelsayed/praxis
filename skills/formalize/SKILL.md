# Formalize

Load [Praxis Control](../../references/control.md).

Work in the inquiry's `shape` position. Begin when the requester selects the
concrete transition from accepted meaning into complete specification or a
progressive working-form strategy. No workflow name is required. Formalize owns
working-form recommendation, composition, and whole-specification
reconciliation. Composed capabilities create and gate their records.

## Bind accepted truth and recommend a route

Read the outcome, accepted Project Brief and Requirements when present,
current source and behavior, references, artifacts, solution context,
uncertainty, change pressure, accepted revisions, persistence boundary, and
caller-retained controls. Do not infer a Project boundary or accepted meaning
from code shape or feature count. Changed needs or constraints return to
Requirements Discovery.

Require each governing Requirement to state one independently understandable
obligation. Substantial research must already be harvested: relevant findings
are either reflected through accepted product meaning, retained for a named
later design or formalization question, kept as genuine uncertainty, rejected,
or incidental. Research evidence becomes a Requirements baseline only through
accepted product meaning.

Recommend exactly one route:

- **standalone Issues** for a bounded outcome that needs durable specification
  but no Project lifecycle;
- **direct Project Issues** when the Project outcome is coherent and several
  independently deliverable behaviors can specify it directly;
- **Evolve** when useful working forms must be selected and learned from in a
  meaningful sequence.

Load [Product Discovery Collaboration](../../references/collaboration.md)
before discussing this product-shape choice.

Explain why the route fits and what it costs in the product's ordinary
language—for example, specifying the complete behavior now versus learning
through a sequence of working forms. Keep internal owner labels out of the
conversation unless they clarify the consequence. The requester chooses the
route. Until then, return `selection-required`; do not manufacture the route
from approval of earlier truth.

## Compose the chosen owner

For standalone or direct Project work, call
[Define Issues](../define-issues/SKILL.md) with the exact accepted outcome,
meaning, boundaries, evidence, persistence location, and ownership. For the
progressive route, call [Evolve](../evolve/SKILL.md) with one accepted Project
boundary and outcome. Preserve the child result, accepted revisions, pending
gate, partial state, and causal return.

Use [Research](../research/SKILL.md), [Interview](../interview/SKILL.md),
[Make Tangible](../make-tangible/SKILL.md),
[Design Solution](../design-solution/SKILL.md), or
[Security](../security/SKILL.md) only when a bounded question can change the
route or specification. They supply evidence or judgment; Formalize retains
synthesis.

Consume child and supporting results without upgrading them:

- Evolve Map, selection, and definition states remain `partial` with their
  exact pending judgment: `map-proposed` or `map-revised` awaits
  `map-approval`; `map-approved` awaits `evolution-selection`;
  `evolution-selected` or `evolution-specified` awaits
  `evolution-definition-approval`. `definition-approved` still requires a separate
  human choice of `issue-definition`. `one-advance` returns for a new direct-
  Issues recommendation; it never switches route.
- Define Issues `defined` or `no-op` completes only the already-selected route;
  `partial` or non-causal `revised` stays partial, while causal, blocked, and
  stopped results remain exact.
- Research `answered` or `no-op`, Interview `clarified`, `qualified` or
  `no-op`, Design Solution `recommended`, `selected`, `qualified` or
  `no-choice`, scoped Security `findings` or `no-high-confidence-findings`,
  Orient `explained`, `understanding-observed`, or
  `understanding-unconfirmed`, and Make Tangible `constructed` artifacts are
  input only. They never complete Formalize.
- Preserve `unverified`, `conflicting`, `deferred`, `needs-evidence`,
  `needs-human-judgment`, `needs-verification`, `needs-owner`, `refused`,
  `orientation-blocked`, `misconception-unresolved`, `blocked`, and `stopped`
  with their exact causal or pending state.

Suggest [Orient](../orient/SKILL.md) only for one settled model whose
comprehension affects the route or specification. Call it only after the requester
accepts that suggestion; a loaded skill, recommendation, or earlier approval
does not select the teaching interaction.

Reconcile the complete translation against accepted truth: observable behavior,
actors, boundaries, exclusions, dependencies, interfaces, failure and
recovery, compatibility, and capable proof. Every Requirement must be covered
or explicitly safe for a named later owner. Return meaning gaps to their
earliest owner rather than filling them with implementation guesses.

For Project work, load [record foundations](../../references/records/foundations.md)
and [discovery records](../../references/records/discovery.md) only when
updating durable navigation. Keep Project Brief navigation terse: chosen route, selected
Evolution or direct outcome, links to governing Map/Evolution/Issues, material
remaining uncertainty, and current recommendation. Requirements and child
records remain authoritative.

Return `formalized` only when the selected route's complete current
specification and all child-owned gates are accepted. An approved Evolution
Definition without Issues is `partial` and requires a separate human choice to
begin Issue definition. Otherwise return `selection-required`, `partial`,
`causal-return`, `blocked`, or `stopped` with route, governing revisions,
child results, semantic coverage, gaps, and recommendation. Recommend Delivery
only after complete specification, then stop.
