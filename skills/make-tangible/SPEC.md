# Make Tangible — Maintainer Contract

## Purpose

Make Tangible turns one caller-owned judgment that is costly to hold in words
into the smallest faithful native artifact or artifact set. It may construct
prose, a table, diagram, image, audio/video, self-contained HTML, executable
probe, dataset, CLI/TUI, report, or repository-native prototype. It owns the
surface's construction, pattern and fidelity choice, revision, intended-medium
verification, and reaction capture. The caller retains the question, accepted
meaning, governing records, gates, authority, interpretation, acceptance, and
stopping.

## Invocation and exclusions

The package is model-invoked because any Praxis owner may need a perceptible
surface and the frontmatter description names that trigger. Host metadata
projects the same capability without adding a trigger, authority, or route.

Entry requires a caller-supplied bounded brief containing the owning question,
audience and starting point, primary judgment, evidence or accepted truth,
consequential fidelity needs, reaction to capture, and authority or lifetime
constraints. A request naming only a medium is incomplete. If ordinary prose,
a short example, or a simple table already makes the judgment easy, the skill
returns `no-op` and performs no construction.

The package excludes product or system judgment, Requirements, Evolutions,
Issues, Plans, Reviews, teaching interpretation, route selection, authority
granting, product adoption, deployment, publication, integration, and
automatic continuation. It does not force HTML, invent alternatives for a
bounded local question, turn artifact interaction into governing truth, or
create record envelopes, artifact manifests, lifecycle ledgers, feedback
ledgers, or learning diaries. Prototype code remains an artifact until an
ordinary Delivery boundary deliberately adopts it.

## Ownership and behavior

Make Tangible owns this bounded sequence:

1. bind the caller's judgment and return a no-op when a surface adds no value;
2. choose exactly one primary judgment pattern, justified supporting patterns,
   medium, and lowest honest fidelity;
3. construct the smallest truthful surface with visible provenance and limits;
4. exercise and verify it through the intended medium;
5. present one exact judged version through its faithful native medium;
6. capture qualified reaction, revise with visible versions, and preserve only
   unaffected reactions; and
7. return one causal message and stop.

The primary pattern classifies the human judgment, not the technology, file
shape, or Praxis phase. Supporting patterns may compose only when they improve
the primary judgment. A pattern reference never owns entry, authority,
completion, route, or continuation.

## Invariants

1. Every run names one caller, one owning question, one primary judgment,
   audience, evidence basis, reaction, and material fidelity limit before
   construction.
2. A no-op request stays conversational and incurs no artifact ceremony.
3. One primary pattern governs fidelity, structure, and verification; the
   pattern is selected from the judgment rather than the medium. Shape
   uncertainty receives genuinely distinct directions before convergence;
   already-bounded questions receive no ceremonial alternatives.
4. The artifact is the lowest fidelity that can support the declared
   conclusion. Behavior that affects the conclusion is real enough; simulated
   behavior and omissions are labeled.
5. Sourced fact, inference, proposal, simulation, and placeholder content are
   visibly distinct and consequential facts trace to exact evidence.
6. Compared candidates use comparable consequential conditions and fidelity,
   or the difference and narrower conclusion are visible.
7. Interaction exists only to experience behavior, explore state, express a
   reaction, or export a composed result; static prose, tables, diagrams, and
   images remain valid surfaces.
8. The intended medium is exercised before reaction. File generation, visual
   polish, or an available screenshot is not verification.
9. A judged version is frozen. A material revision receives a new visible
   version; unaffected reactions may carry forward only while their basis is
   valid. Every revised version is re-exercised, re-presented, and receives a
   new reaction before the `revised` outcome.
10. Reactions preserve mixed choices, rejected parts, annotations, edits,
    qualifications, deferred answers, and unresolved points; they do not
    collapse to one winner.
11. Plannotator Reaction Review is used only when its native surface preserves
    the exact target and materially improves the intended judgment; another
    native interaction requires prior caller selection. It owns presentation
    and transport, not artifact construction, interpretation, or authority.
    Dismissal or empty closure maps to `stopped`; tool or transport failure maps
    to `blocked`; neither produces a reaction or acceptance.
12. Manage Worktree is used only for a named repository collision, preservation,
    rollback, parallel-editor, or experiment risk; a worktree does not isolate
    external resources or grant mutation authority.
13. Artifacts and reactions are transient by default. Retain exact bytes only
    for a named future consumer; the return names its owner, path, exact
    version, reaction export, disposition, and preservation or removal
    evidence. Artifacts remain native and receive no structured-record
    envelope or lifecycle ledger.
14. A reaction that changes accepted meaning returns to its causal owner. Make
    Tangible never absorbs governing revision or chooses the next route.
15. Every outcome returns the judgment, evidence, limits, implications,
    unresolved points, and retention state, then stops. `constructed` and
    `revised` also require an exact artifact/version and captured reaction;
    `no-op` names the sufficient direct surface, `blocked` names the missing
    boundary and preserved state, and `stopped` names current state without
    inferring reaction.

## Direct composition

### Plannotator

When a verified target is faithful to a native Plannotator surface and a
native reaction materially improves the judgment, call
[Plannotator](../plannotator/SPEC.md) for exactly one Reaction Review, or for a
native interaction the caller has already selected. Supply the exact
target/version, requested judgment, and what it does not authorize. Preserve
its native decision, annotations, dismissal, closure, reference, and fidelity
or transport limits. Make Tangible does not choose a Record Gate or Result
Gate for the caller. A failure remains a failure; no conversational approval
substitutes for a required native gate. Make Tangible retains the artifact,
primary judgment, construction, verification, interpretation, and stopping.
Map a captured review result into the bounded revision/return loop. Map
dismissal or empty closure to `stopped` with no inferred reaction or
acceptance. Map tool or transport failure to `blocked` with exact target and
limit; do not synthesize a reaction, revision, approval, or fallback
interaction. A native path may continue only when it was independently selected
before the failure and remains faithful; it is not a substituted fallback.

### Manage Worktree

When a named repository-native prototype risk could be removed by checkout
isolation, call [Manage Worktree](../manage-worktree/SPEC.md). Supply the risk,
base, destination, mutation surfaces, and current authority. Accept only its
bounded checkout result and limits; it does not authorize construction or
external-resource action. If it returns `blocked` or `stopped`, preserve state
and return without construction or cleanup.

## Effects, authority, persistence, and lifetime

Permitted effects are read-only inspection, disposable local artifact
construction, intended-medium exercise, reaction capture, and ordinary local
revision inside the caller's current authority. A repository-native prototype
may require the caller to establish a Manage Worktree boundary first.
Product mutation, external effects, data/secret access, service or container
changes, consequential experiments, and other effectful work retain their
normal owner and require their normal exact authority and safety boundary.
Make Tangible never treats a visual reaction, Plannotator approval, or exported
proposal as Implementation Authorization.

Conversation and native interaction are transient by default. Persist only the
exact artifact or reaction a named future consumer needs, using the lifecycle
reference and the caller's owning records. If no persistence declaration names
a durable consumer, keep the return as a message. Do not create a generic
artifact index, lifecycle receipt, or trajectory record.

## Return and outcomes

The package returns exactly one of:

- `no-op`: the judgment is already easy without a tangible surface;
- `constructed`: a first verified surface supports the declared judgment;
- `revised`: a new judged version incorporates local reaction while preserving
  version identity and unaffected reactions;
- `blocked`: brief, fidelity, medium, authority, dependency, or verification
  boundary prevents an honest surface;
- `stopped`: the caller or human ended the bounded action.

The native return is:

```text
Make Tangible Result {
  outcome,
  artifact_path_and_version?,
  primary_judgment,
  medium_and_fidelity,
  supported_conclusions,
  human_reaction?,
  decisive_evidence,
  implications,
  fidelity_or_transport_limits,
  unresolved_points,
  retention_state {
    disposition,
    named_consumer?,
    owner_and_path?,
    exact_version?,
    reaction_export?,
    preservation_or_removal_evidence,
  },
}
```

`constructed` and `revised` require the exact artifact/version, declared
judgment, exercised behavior, provenance, evidence, reaction, supported
conclusions, limits, and retention decision. `no-op` explains the direct
surface that already suffices. `blocked` names the missing boundary and
preserves any created checkout or artifact state. `stopped` preserves all
current state and does not infer cleanup. Every return states that the caller
retains governing meaning, authority, gate consequence, acceptance, route, and
stopping. A lifecycle return names its consumer, owner/path, exact version,
reaction export, disposition, and verified preservation or removal; a
non-persistent return is explicitly transient or disposable.

## All-package conformance baseline

1. Frontmatter, host metadata, body, contract, effects, invocation class,
   dependencies, return, and stop agree.
2. The no-op branch performs no construction or rich-surface ceremony.
3. Every branch has one primary judgment and one direct context pointer when
   conditional guidance is needed; no reference chain is used.
4. Artifacts are truthful at the declared fidelity and distinguish provenance.
5. The intended medium is exercised before reaction and its limits are reported.
6. Judged versions are frozen; material changes are visible new versions.
7. Mixed, qualified, rejected, deferred, and unresolved reactions survive
   export and remain bound to exact versions.
8. Plannotator and Manage Worktree are invoked only at their named decision
   points, with ownership and authority retained by Make Tangible's caller.
9. Artifact retention is exact, lazy, named-consumer-driven, and native.
10. Blocked, stopped, no-op, constructed, and revised outcomes stay distinct;
    each return includes evidence and limitations and performs no adjacent work.

## Representative conformance cases

1. **Prose no-op:** a short clear explanation returns `no-op` without HTML or
   prototype construction.
2. **Every primary pattern:** each of the eleven judgment patterns loads only
   its named reference and returns the reference's required evidence and limits.
3. **One primary with support:** a multi-pattern surface names one governing
   judgment; supporting patterns do not change ownership or return meaning.
4. **Shape uncertainty:** unresolved solution shape receives genuinely
   distinct directions before convergence; an already-bounded question gets
   one surface rather than ceremonial alternatives.
5. **Fidelity boundary:** a simulated surrounding service is labeled and does
   not claim latency, reliability, or feasibility it did not exercise.
6. **Provenance:** sourced facts, inferences, proposals, simulations, and
   placeholders are locally distinguishable and consequential sources resolve.
7. **Comparable alternatives:** candidates share consequential conditions or
   the unequal fidelity narrows the supported conclusion.
8. **Native verification:** the artifact works in its intended medium,
   including required states, links, responsive/theme/accessibility behavior,
   and reaction/export path.
9. **Plannotator-compatible surface:** when native review materially improves
   judgment, one exact target is presented. A captured result may continue;
   dismissal or empty closure returns `stopped`, and failure returns `blocked`,
   with no inferred reaction or acceptance.
10. **Incompatible surface:** essential behavior stays in its native medium;
   the artifact is not redesigned merely to fit Plannotator.
11. **Versioned reaction:** after a judged version, a material revision gets a
    new visible version, is re-exercised and re-presented, and captures a new
    reaction before returning `revised`; unaffected reactions alone carry
    forward.
12. **Composed reaction:** mixed choices, annotations, edits, qualifications,
    and unresolved points export against the exact version.
13. **Lifecycle:** retention, revision, transfer, or removal loads the
    lifecycle reference; no named consumer means message-only return. A
    persistent result names consumer, owner/path, exact version, reaction
    export, disposition, and preservation or removal evidence.
14. **Worktree risk:** a named repository collision invokes Manage Worktree;
    absent risk does not create isolation, and blocked/stopped state is kept.
15. **Unsupported conclusion:** when the medium or fidelity cannot support the
    requested judgment, return `blocked` with the evidence and missing change;
    do not ask the human to infer it.

The package has no unresolved design decision. Release requires exact tree,
frontmatter/YAML validity, plugin-local links, no forbidden external runtime
references, CSS fidelity, reference standalone behavior, and conformance to
the cases above.
