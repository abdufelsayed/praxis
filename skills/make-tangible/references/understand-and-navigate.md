# Understand and Navigate

Load only when the judgment requires a mental model of dense systems, domains,
code paths, ownership, state transitions, or evidence. Stop when the reader
can locate, explain, and reason about the relationships relevant to the
question; do not document everything.

## Shape the model

Lead with actors, boundaries, and flow. Provide progressive paths to the
needed ownership/responsibility, interfaces/contracts, states/transitions,
causal or temporal flow, exceptions/failures, terminology, and exact source.
Use stable labels and links between overview and evidence. A repository dump or
flat file list is not a mental model.

Trace consequential nodes and claims locally to current source or evidence.
Label inferred, proposed, simulated, unavailable, and unknown relationships.
Keep current and proposed systems visibly distinct. Add search, filtering,
collapsible detail, layers, synchronized source, or breadcrumbs only when
they reduce memory/search burden; keep scope and hidden evidence visible.

Inspection, observation, experience, elicitation, teaching, or proof may
support the model when they reduce a specific comprehension gap; this pattern
remains primary while the purpose is forming and navigating the mental model.

Invite correction of missing actors, ownership, relationships, ordering,
terminology, failure paths, unsupported inference, and unexplained routes.
Do not require a quiz merely to prove reading. Return the confirmed model,
corrections, evidence, remaining gaps and fidelity limits to the caller.

## Verify

Check that the overview answers the question, overview and drill-down agree,
consequential paths are followable end to end, labels remain consistent,
current/inferred/proposed/simulated/unknown truth is distinct, source links
resolve, exceptions are not hidden, navigation preserves scope, and the
intended medium is usable and accessible. Return the exact version and the
implication for the owning question; the caller decides whether to research,
interview, teach, or continue.
