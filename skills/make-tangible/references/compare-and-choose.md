# Compare and Choose

Load only when the requester understands the decision and consequential criteria
well enough to judge credible alternatives. If the requester is still discovering
what matters, the primary judgment is discovery rather than selection.

## Build a fair comparison

Show the smallest candidate set representing the real decision. Candidates are
materially distinct, credible under accepted constraints, and strong enough to
deserve consideration. Include the incumbent or no-change option when viable;
state meaningful exclusions and reasons. Derive criteria from the owning
question and accepted truth. Keep hard constraints as pass/fail boundaries,
separate tradeoffs and human preferences, and expose evidence maturity and
unknowns. Use scores only when scale, weighting, and aggregation have a
defensible meaning.

Use the same consequential inputs, scale, states, constraints, and measurement
method where possible. Label what is measured, inferred, speculative, or
simulated. Show unavoidable fidelity or evidence differences and narrow the
conclusion when comparison is not like-for-like. Recommend the best-supported
candidate, its sacrifice, remaining uncertainty, and the condition that would
change the recommendation. If evidence cannot support a choice, recommend the
smallest discriminating probe.

Understanding, experience, inspection, manipulation, proof, or elicitation
may support the comparison when it improves a consequential contrast; this
pattern remains primary while the purpose is deciding among understood
alternatives.

## Capture and verify

Allow selection, combination, rejection of one or all, conditions or
thresholds, fallback, deferral pending named evidence, and reframing. Export
the exact compared versions, decisive criteria, qualifications, tradeoffs,
fallbacks, unresolved evidence, and recommendation when conversation is
insufficient. Before presentation, verify candidate credibility/distinction,
incumbent coverage, hard constraints, provenance, comparable conditions,
evidence maturity, visible reasoning, and qualified reaction export.

Return the exact version, decision and qualifications, evidence, accepted
tradeoffs, rejected candidates and reasons, unresolved evidence or probe, and
change condition. The caller interprets the result and owns the gate and any
later authority.
