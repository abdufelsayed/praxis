# Observe and Assess

Load only when measured state, change, trends, clusters, or anomalies need to
become judgeable. This surface does not diagnose, prove correctness, verify a
result, or accept a claim.

## Preserve observation context

Show source and collection method, time/freshness, environment, population and
denominator, baseline/comparison window, filters/grouping, sampling or
aggregation, missing/excluded/failed/unavailable data, and measurement limits.
Do not combine runs or populations without making aggregation visible.

Organize around the owning question. Preserve absolute values, denominator,
baseline, direction/magnitude, variability, thresholds and source, and a path
from summaries to underlying cases. Use a scale that does not exaggerate weak
signals. Keep measured state, derived aggregation, anomaly/pattern, causal
hypothesis, recommended probe, and caller finding distinct. Show evidence that
supports and conflicts with a hypothesis.

Keep active query, filters, grouping, population, hidden/excluded counts, and
drill-down scope visible. Export exact version and query/context when needed.
Understanding, inspection, manipulation, communication, proof, or elicitation
may support the surface when they clarify the observed shape; this pattern
remains primary while measured evidence itself is the judgment.
Verify counts, denominators, values, timestamps, environments, baselines,
filters, drill-downs, scales, evidence links, and export. Return observations,
patterns, hypotheses, missing/stale/conflicting evidence, limits, recommended
probe, and implication; the caller owns diagnosis or verification.
