# Explore and Discover

Load only when the primary judgment is discovering vocabulary, criteria,
preferences, blind spots, promising directions, or a better question. The
result need not rank, converge, or select one winner.

## Build the possibility space

Start with the owning question and expose the few dimensions that could change
understanding. Present a small set of coherent directions, each a meaningful
hypothesis rather than a cosmetic variation. Stop when another direction
would not reveal a materially different idea or criterion. Preserve the
incumbent or no-change direction when it is genuinely useful to discovery.

Keep representative content shape, scale, constraints, normal/empty/loading/
failure/boundary states, and the dimensions under reaction. Label invented
data, speculative behavior, simulated mechanics, and omissions. Unequal polish
or fidelity may provoke a reaction, but cannot support a comparable conclusion
unless the difference and narrower scope are explicit.

## Invite discovery

Let the human keep, reject, combine, question, correct, defer, or ask to
explore further. Preserve free-form reaction; a single-choice control cannot
represent mixed reactions. Record newly named criteria, surprises, blind spots,
false assumptions, promising and unhelpful directions, and why where known.
Export those reactions against the exact judged version when conversation is
not reliable. The export is evidence for the caller, never an accepted
Requirement, Evolution, Issue, Plan, or authority.

Supporting comparison, elicitation, experience, manipulation, or navigation
may be composed when it reveals the possibility space; this pattern remains
primary while the purpose is learning what matters rather than selecting a
known alternative.

## Verify and return

Before presentation, verify directions differ on the declared dimensions,
constraints and states are represented, epistemic labels are clear, conditions
are comparable or visibly limited, no order/default/polish silently ranks a
direction, all controls work in the intended medium, and mixed/free-form
reactions export accurately. Return the exact version, discovered vocabulary
and criteria, reactions and qualifications, fidelity limits, unresolved
questions, and the implication for the caller. The caller decides whether to
continue discovery, compare, investigate, or revise governing meaning.
