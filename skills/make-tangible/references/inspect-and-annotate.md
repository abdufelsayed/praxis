# Inspect and Annotate

Load only when exact evidence needs evidence-bound scrutiny and reaction:
source, diff, trace, screenshot, risk, finding, design system, or test result.
Review, Research, Debug, Verify, or another caller owns the judgment; this
surface does not declare a defect, promote a note, or grant review authority.

## Bind and qualify annotations

Every consequential annotation names the artifact/evidence version, stable
location/region/event/state, visible excerpt and context, and supporting links
where needed. If evidence changes, mark the annotation stale and show changed
context; never silently relocate it. Distinguish observed fact, inferred
concern, question, correction, suggestion, human reaction, caller finding,
dispute, and unresolved point. Preserve severity, confidence, and
qualification when supplied.

Keep inspected scope, included/excluded/filtered/stale/unavailable evidence,
active grouping, and hidden counts visible. The absence of a note is not an
approval or review claim. Export each material annotation with exact anchor and
version, wording/type, qualification, related evidence, response/correction,
and status when conversation cannot safely preserve it.

Understanding, observation, elicitation, manipulation, communication, or proof
may support the surface when they improve evidence-bound scrutiny; this pattern
remains primary while exact evidence and anchored reaction are the judgment.

Verify anchors, excerpts, links, scope, filters, navigation, stale handling,
status distinctions, and export round-trip. Return exact version and scope,
all evidence-bound reactions and statuses, corrections, stale/disputed/
unresolved evidence, limits, and implication. The caller decides findings,
changes, gates, and records.
