# Elicit and Articulate

Load only when concrete context is needed for latent human requirements,
preferences, constraints, corrections, or contextual knowledge. The reaction
is inspectable evidence; it is not governing truth.

## Ask consequential questions

Interview or another caller owns conversational question selection,
recommendation, exchange, and interpretation. This surface supplies only the
concrete behavior, evidence, comparison, or manipulation needed to express a
missing human judgment. Each question must materially affect the owning work,
sit beside the relevant context, and state its consequence. Order dependent
questions so earlier answers simplify later ones.

State recommendations explicitly and present credible responses fairly. Permit
selection, combination, custom wording, correction, uncertainty, deferral, and
rejection of every framing. Keep accepted/source context, recommendation,
human response, simulation/placeholder, unanswered, deferred, and invalidated
meaning distinct. Never use defaults, unequal polish, or loaded wording as a
hidden recommendation.

Bind each response to exact artifact version, visible state, question,
conditions, audience, qualifications, and premise corrections. When a revised
state changes a dependent answer, reopen that question and preserve unaffected
answers; never export stale answers as current. Export all contextual meaning
when conversation is insufficient.

Exploration, comparison, experience, manipulation, inspection, or teaching may
support the surface when they expose the context needed for expression; this
pattern remains primary while the purpose is stating latent human meaning.

## Verify and return

Verify every question is consequential and current, consequences and
recommendations are fair, mixed/custom/qualified responses survive, dependent
answers reopen, and export preserves context and status. Return expressed
requirements/preferences/constraints/corrections, conditions, recommendation
and response, invalidated/deferred/uncertain questions, newly discovered
criteria or false premises, and implication. The caller promotes only
supported meaning and owns every record or gate.
