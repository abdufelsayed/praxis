# Artifact lifecycle

Load this reference when a Make Tangible artifact is retained, revised after a
judgment, presented across a context boundary, or removed. A disposable
one-turn artifact returned and discarded needs no lifecycle handling.

## Contract

An artifact is something a person can inspect, run, hear, measure, compare, or
react to: a diagram, HTML surface, prototype, CLI/TUI, image, audio/video,
simulation, experiment result, benchmark, dataset, annotated diff, editor, or
report. It is evidence or a reaction surface, not a decision.

Every retained artifact exposes the question, intended audience, primary
judgment, sourced/inferred/proposed/simulated/placeholder status, material
fidelity limits, and requested or preserved reaction. Put this frame inside
the artifact when the medium supports it; otherwise place it beside the exact
artifact link. Mark provenance at the point where it changes interpretation
and link consequential facts to their exact source.

## Version and revision

Before consequential reaction, a working artifact may be revised in place.
Once a version is judged, freeze that exact version. A material revision gets a
new visible version, such as `artifacts/auth-flow-v2.html` or
`artifacts/auth-flow/v2/`. Keep the judged version reachable from the selected
destination. Carry unaffected reactions forward only when their basis remains
valid and reopen changed or invalidated judgments.

Export interaction when conversation cannot reconstruct it reliably. The
export names the artifact version and preserves selections, rejected parts,
mixed choices, edits, annotations, qualifications, and unresolved points. Use
the native format that fits the surface. Add a manifest only when the artifact's
consumer and lifecycle require one.

## Storage and retention

Artifacts start as disposable working outputs. Retain exact bytes only when a
named later activity needs their behavior, evidence, comparison, or reaction.
Keep the smallest useful judged version. If `.praxis` is the selected home,
place the artifact under the relevant effort rather than creating a global
artifact collection. Use the requester's named destination when one exists.

Move the complete artifact and update links only when the requester changes its
owning effort or destination. Remove retained bytes only after no named consumer
needs the exact form.
Plannotator may carry a reaction, but it does not change the artifact's owner or
turn the reaction into a decision.

## Safe removal

Before removal, identify the exact path and version, named consumers, recovery,
and effect. Preserve a version when its reaction, evidence, comparison, or
future consumer is still needed. Remove only the selected unneeded bytes.
Consequential loss and external cleanup remain separate actions.

## Completion

Lifecycle work is complete when the selected retention, move, or removal is
verified and the exact version, named consumer, recovery, and applicable
reaction export are known. Return only the facts and limitations that apply.
