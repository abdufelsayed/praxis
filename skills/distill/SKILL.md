# Distill

Write the operational truth, not the archaeology.

## Rules

- Preserve current intent.
- Preserve decisions and live constraints.
- Preserve open questions and blockers.
- Drop discarded options unless future agents are likely to repeat them.
- Do not write self-referential process commentary.
- Do not frame artifacts around what was ignored.

Choose the target artifact first: context, decision, spec, issue, plan, or handoff.
