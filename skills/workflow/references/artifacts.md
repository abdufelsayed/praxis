# Praxis Artifacts

Durable artifacts are distilled operational state, not transcripts.

## Artifact Types

- `context`: current project/session state, goals, decisions, open questions, next actions.
- `discussion`: useful exploration that may matter later.
- `decision`: final choice, rationale, consequences, and only important rejected alternatives.
- `spec`: desired behavior and constraints.
- `issue`: durable work item with acceptance criteria.
- `plan`: executable steps for current work.
- `handoff`: compact continuation note.

## Hygiene

- Do not preserve every discarded idea.
- Do not write plans as ledgers of what was ignored.
- Mention rejected options only when future agents are likely to repeat the mistake.
- Prefer the living intent over the conversation archaeology.
- Keep artifacts useful for a fresh agent that has no transcript.
