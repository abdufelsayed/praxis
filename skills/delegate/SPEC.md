# Delegate — Maintainer Contract

## Purpose

Delegate coordinates one bounded subagent investigation, implementation,
review, or verification while the supervisor retains the human relationship,
governing context, judgment, authority, integration, acceptance, and final
claims. Delegation is primarily context routing; it may also reduce total cost
or elapsed time when the work genuinely divides. It is not a mandatory worker
pipeline, a general instruction to fan work out, or a transfer of ownership.

## Invocation, inputs, and exclusions

Delegate is model-invoked because any owning skill may need bounded independent
work when the expected context, cost, elapsed-time, or evidence benefit is
positive. Its model-facing description and host projection are part of this
contract. Model selection alone is not behavioral entry; the capability begins
only with a bounded outcome and a usable delegation brief. A direct human
request can also begin it when that request supplies the same boundary.

The supervisor or human supplies, or makes discoverable:

- one bounded question or outcome and its current owning loop;
- accepted truth and consequential context needed for judgment;
- scope, exclusions, mutation surfaces, and required behavior or claims;
- exact authority, tools, permissions, safety boundaries, and secret limits;
- required proof and authoritative starting sources;
- return and escalation conditions; and
- the intended role, model capability, context-isolation path, or the
  information needed to choose them.

Missing outcome, scope, exclusions, proof, source, authority, or exact
mutation boundary is a blocked input, not a reason to guess. A dirty checkout,
large task, another agent, or tidiness preference alone is not a delegation or
worktree trigger.

Delegate excludes product or system judgment, Requirements, Formalization,
route selection, human gates, acceptance, architecture ownership,
supervisor-facing synthesis, authority creation, automatic progression,
mandatory fan-out, hidden global routing, broad project management, and
unbounded implementation. It does not silently grant correction, another Plan,
commit, push, merge, deployment, publication, external effect, or cleanup.

## Behavioral entry and retained ownership

Evaluate the benefit before launch. The capability may improve:

1. supervisor-context quality by isolating noisy searches, logs, discarded
   paths, or independent reasoning;
2. total cost by routing work to the cheapest capable model after briefing,
   correction, integration, and assurance; or
3. elapsed time by running genuinely independent evidence paths or mutations
   concurrently when coordination and integration cost less than the time
   saved.

A meaningful improvement in one dimension is sufficient; none is assumed.
Quick targeted work, frequent human back-and-forth, and tightly dependent
reasoning remain in the supervisor context unless another benefit clearly
outweighs handoff cost. The negative-benefit outcome is `no-op` and performs no
launch or delegation ceremony.

The supervisor owns human intent, current Praxis owner, accepted Requirements,
Evolutions, Issues, Plans, consequential design context, decomposition,
priorities, architecture, interfaces, authority, integration, human-facing
recommendations and gates, acceptance, and the decision to correct,
backtrack, escalate, or stop. A delegate owns only the bounded function named
in its brief. A message, fork, worktree, result, or handoff carries state and
evidence but never transfers authority or governing truth.

## Lifecycle

Delegate's bounded lifecycle is:

1. establish one real benefit or return `no-op`;
2. write the complete brief and retain ownership;
3. inspect checkout and shared-resource boundaries;
4. choose the cheapest capable role and runtime path;
5. launch a fresh least-privilege context;
6. supervise by exception and correct, restart, or return as needed;
7. evaluate independent returns when the condition is present; and
8. compact the result, persist only named truth, and stop.

The package may combine one coherent role's functions when that preserves
independence and evidence quality. It does not split work merely to satisfy
role names, and one role does not create a second delegate by default.

## Invariants

1. Delegation begins only for one bounded outcome with a positive expected
   context, cost, elapsed-time, or evidence benefit; negative benefit is a
   `no-op` with no launch.
2. The delegation brief names outcome, accepted truth and consequential
   context, scope and exclusions, authority/tools/safety, required behavior or
   claims, required proof, authoritative sources, return/escalation
   conditions, and requested result shape.
3. A brief describes only a bounded subset of a current human grant. It never
   widens the supervisor's authority or transfers the human relationship,
   governing meaning, gates, acceptance, or stopping.
4. Fresh isolated context is the default. A bounded fork is used only when
   essential conversational meaning cannot be transferred faithfully and
   economically; delegation is one level deep by default.
5. Before concurrent mutation, actual overlap in files, indexes, generated
   state, processes, or other resources is inspected. One active mutating
   owner is used when overlap is possible; read-only concurrency cannot alter
   shared state.
6. A separate checkout is considered only for a named subagent collision or
   preservation risk. Manage Worktree owns that checkout decision and
   lifecycle; Delegate retains task division, supervision, synthesis,
   authority, integration, and stopping.
7. Worktree isolation separates checkout state only. Databases, services,
   ports, processes, caches, credentials, containers, remote state, and
   infrastructure remain shared unless separately isolated and authorized.
8. Role selection follows bounded work: Scout traces repository truth,
   Researcher investigates external evidence, Worker performs bounded
   implementation, Reviewer adversarially examines a fixed target, Verifier
   tests exact claims, and Oracle supplies exceptional supervisor-grade
   judgment. Roles are not stages.
9. Runtime selection uses the cheapest capable model and escalates for
   ambiguity, architectural judgment, blast radius, weak proof, repeated
   failure, or likely correction cost. Host-native delegation is preferred
   when available; direct execution is an honest fallback when feasible and
   does not change the brief or authority.
10. A Worker receives only the exact mutation subset already covered by the
    human's current Implementation Authorization. The supervisor understands
    the gray-box boundary, observable behavior, failure semantics, interfaces,
    invariants, side effects, compatibility, exclusions, and proof before
    delegating implementation. Only Worker may receive product-mutation
    authority; other roles remain read-only with respect to product state,
    except for explicitly authorized disposable Verifier proof artifacts.
11. Destructive, irreversible, production, data-affecting,
    permission-changing, secret-handling, cross-project, or external actions
    require the same explicit authority and safety boundary as in the main
    thread. A delegate without that authority returns before the action.
12. Delegates work quietly and communicate by exception: material blockers or
    missing inputs, governing conflicts, safety or authority concerns, needed
    supervisor decisions, or evidence that should cancel or redirect work.
13. If truth and the brief remain valid and correction is local, the same
    delegate may resume. A material brief change, polluted context, needed
    independent judgment, misunderstanding, or excessive correction/integration
    cost requires a fresh delegate, escalation, or supervisor return.
14. When any governing truth or authority boundary is invalidated, affected
    authority becomes non-active; affected mutating delegates stop at the
    earliest safe point, make current state safe, and return partial state and
    evidence. Independent read-only work may continue only when unaffected by
    the invalidated premise, and learning returns to the exact causal owner.
15. Shared evaluation loads only when several delegates pursue distinct
    evidence paths or returns conflict. It keeps feedback bound to target and
    evaluator context and preserves disagreement; a single contained delegate
    does not load it.
16. A completion report is evidence, not proof. The supervisor inspects the
    integrated result, governing boundary, material changes or findings, and
    decisive evidence before making a human-facing claim.
17. Persistence is message-first and transient. A durable projection is made
    only when a named future consumer needs it and is promoted into the record
    owned by the relevant loop; no transcript, status file, handoff ledger,
    scorecard, or generic delegation history is created.
18. Completion returns a compact result or honest blocker with exact evidence,
    material changes, verification, limitations, unresolved questions, and a
    recommendation when supported; it then stops without adjacent work or new
    authority.

## Outcomes and observable return

Delegate exposes these semantic outcomes where applicable:

- `delegated`: the bounded role returned a usable result and evidence;
- `no-op`: direct work is the better path and no delegate was launched;
- `partial`: a safe incomplete state and evidence returned after an early
  stop, invalidated premise, or bounded failure;
- `blocked`: missing brief, authority, safety, resource, isolation, or
  execution dependency prevented a usable launch or action; or
- `stopped`: the human or supervisor ended the bounded delegation.

These labels are semantic return guidance, not a required serialized envelope.
Every outcome names limitations and the missing human or supervisor judgment
when the result is not complete. A usable return contains, as applicable:

```text
Delegate Result {
  outcome,
  role,
  result,
  exact_evidence_and_source_pointers,
  material_findings_or_changes?,
  verification_performed?,
  limitations_and_confidence,
  unresolved_questions_or_deviations,
  recommended_next_step
}
```

The result includes exact changed paths or integration pointers for mutation,
decisive proof and its signal, and safe partial state when work stopped early.
It says that the supervisor retains interpretation, integration, acceptance,
authority, and stopping. A recommendation appears only when evidence
supports one; otherwise it identifies the missing evidence or judgment.

## Effects, authority, persistence, and lifetime

Effects are limited to proportionate delegation setup and communication,
read-only inspection, and the bounded role's work explicitly covered by the
supervisor's current authority. Host access is not acceptance authority.
Delegation never amplifies permissions. A Worker may receive a bounded subset
of active Plan authority, but the human instruction remains its source and the
supervisor retains integration and judgment.

The supervisor or delegated role applies the ordinary consequence boundary to
each action. Destructive, irreversible, production, data, permission, secret,
cross-project, infrastructure, or other consequential actions require exact
Plan and implementation authority and the same safety handling as in the main
thread. A role lacking that boundary returns before acting. Delegate does not
use a result, worktree, model, or brief to manufacture authority.

The return is conversational by default. Promote only interpreted truth that
a named future owner or gate needs into the narrowest owning record. Raw
transcripts, logs, status files, and coordination records remain transient.
Delegation ends when the result/evidence or an honest blocker has returned;
the supervisor decides correction, integration, acceptance, persistence,
forward work, and stopping.

## Invocation and host projection

The package is model-invoked because bounded owning skills need autonomous
reach after a genuine benefit and brief exist. Its frontmatter is:

```yaml
---
name: delegate
description: "Delegate one bounded question or outcome. Use when isolated context, independent evidence, parallel work, or a cheaper capable agent provides net benefit."
---
```

The host projection is presentation and launch copy only:

```yaml
interface:
  display_name: "Delegate"
  short_description: "Assign bounded work without transferring ownership"
  default_prompt: "Use $delegate to assign this bounded work under the stated authority."
policy:
  allow_implicit_invocation: true
```

Host metadata cannot add a trigger, effect, dependency, authority, route, or
continuation.

## All-package conformance baseline

1. A positive benefit and one bounded outcome are established before launch;
   negative benefit returns `no-op` without ceremony.
2. `SKILL.md`, `SPEC.md`, and host metadata agree on model invocation, genuine
   triggers, exclusions, authority, effects, return, and stopping.
3. The brief contains every material truth, boundary, source, proof, and
   escalation condition required to distinguish local judgment from a
   consequential unknown.
4. Fresh context is normal, forks are exceptional, delegation is one level
   deep, and host fallback preserves the same bounded contract.
5. Shared files, indexes, generated state, processes, and external resources
   are inspected before concurrent mutation; one mutating owner is used when
   overlap is possible.
6. Manage Worktree is invoked only for a named repository isolation risk, and
   its exact return and retained ownership are preserved.
7. Role choice and model capability match the brief; Worker gray-box
   boundaries and mutation authority are understood before implementation.
8. Missing authority or safety boundaries stop the affected delegate before
   action; delegated access never becomes acceptance or continuation authority.
9. Quiet exception communication, correction versus restart, early safe stop,
   causal return, and independent read-only continuation follow the active
   governing truth.
10. Evaluation is loaded only for distinct evidence paths or conflicts and
    does not collapse feedback into a score.
11. Durable persistence is named-consumer-only and record-owned; no delegation
    transcript, ledger, or automatic learning store appears.
12. Every non-no-op outcome returns compact evidence, changes or findings,
    proof, limits, unresolved state, and a supported recommendation, then
    stops without adjacent work.

## Package-specific conformance cases

1. **Negative benefit:** a quick contained edit, frequent human exchange, or
   tightly dependent reasoning remains direct and returns `no-op` without
   launching a role.
2. **Context isolation:** noisy repository exploration is delegated in fresh
   context with a bounded brief; the supervisor retains synthesis and does
   not import raw logs or discarded paths.
3. **Independent review:** distinct evidence paths are delegated only when
   contrast can change the judgment; evaluation preserves evaluator context,
   disagreement, and the smallest resolving probe.
4. **Parallel but coupled work:** overlapping mutation uses one active owner;
   apparently parallel tasks are sequenced or isolated after actual resource
   inspection rather than by role count.
5. **Cheapest capable role:** a Scout, Researcher, Worker, Reviewer, or
   Verifier uses the cheapest capable model; ambiguity, consequence, weak
   proof, repeated failure, or correction cost causes escalation; Oracle is
   reserved for consequential disagreement or drift.
6. **Gray-box boundary:** a Worker receives understood behavior, interfaces,
   invariants, side effects, compatibility, exclusions, exact mutation scope,
   and proof; a missing consequential decision returns rather than being
   invented.
7. **Read-only versus mutating authority:** Scout, Researcher, Reviewer,
   Verifier, and Oracle remain read-only by normal definition; Worker mutates
   only within the exact human-authorized subset and cannot accept the result.
8. **Worker overreach:** an attempted scope expansion, destructive action,
   external effect, or governing redesign stops at the safe point and returns
   evidence and impact without integration.
9. **Exception reporting:** routine progress stays quiet; a material blocker,
   conflict, safety concern, needed decision, or redirecting evidence returns
   compactly to the supervisor.
10. **Contradictory delegates:** evaluator signals remain tied to target,
    evidence, conditions, and evaluator context; disagreement is returned to
    the earliest owner rather than averaged or majority-voted away.
11. **Correction versus restart:** local correction resumes a delegate only
    while brief and governing truth remain valid; material brief change,
    polluted context, independent judgment, misunderstanding, or excessive
    integration cost uses a fresh delegate or supervisor return.
12. **Worktree isolation:** a named subagent collision or preservation risk
    invokes Manage Worktree; its safe-current, ready, handoff, cleanup,
    blocked, or stopped return stays within Delegate's supervision and never
    transfers authority or claims broader resource isolation.
13. **Supervisor-owned acceptance:** a returned result is evidence; the
    supervisor inspects the fixed target and decisive proof, interprets
    learning, obtains any human judgment, and alone accepts or continues.
14. **Invalidated governing truth or authority:** affected authority becomes
    non-active, mutating delegates stop at the earliest safe point, make state
    safe, and return partial evidence; independent read-only work continues
    only when its premise is unaffected, and learning returns to its owner.
15. **Compact return and persistence:** exact paths, proof, limits, unresolved
    questions, and recommendation return in one usable message; only named
    future-consumer truth is promoted to its owning record.

The package is accepted only when the exact four-file tree, final plugin-local
links, surface reconciliation, and bounded fresh-context behavior support
these claims.
