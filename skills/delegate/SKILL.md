# Delegate

Load [Praxis Control](../../references/control.md).

Delegation is a context boundary, not an ownership transfer or mandatory
pipeline. The supervisor keeps human context, governing meaning, authority,
integration, judgment, and the final claim.

## Prove a benefit and write the brief

Delegate only when the outcome is bounded and at least one benefit survives
briefing, correction, integration, and assurance cost: protecting supervisor
context from noisy work; using a cheaper capable model; obtaining genuinely
independent evidence; or reducing elapsed time through independent work. A
small targeted change or work needing frequent human exchange usually stays
direct. Return `no-op` when delegation has no net benefit.

Before launch, fix the outcome, accepted truth and consequential context,
scope and exclusions, authority and safety, required behavior or claims,
proof, authoritative starting pointers, return and escalation conditions, and
requested result. Missing essentials are `blocked`, never delegated guesses.

Inspect overlapping files, indexes, generated state, processes, services,
ports, caches, credentials, and external resources. Use one mutating owner
where overlap is possible. Call [Manage Worktree](../manage-worktree/SKILL.md)
only for a concrete checkout collision, preservation, or rollback risk; a
worktree isolates files, not shared services or remote state.

## Choose and supervise the capable role

- **Scout** traces local ownership, behavior, and source evidence.
- **Researcher** answers a bounded external or domain question.
- **Worker** implements one understood gray-box boundary.
- **Reviewer** challenges one fixed target.
- **Verifier** tests exact claims.
- **Oracle** gives independent supervisor-grade judgment on consequential
  direction, drift, contradiction, or unresolved disagreement.

Load [role guidance](references/roles.md) when selection, authority, evidence,
return, or escalation is not obvious. Choose the cheapest capable path. Use a
fresh isolated context by default and one delegation level unless the requester or
host contract explicitly calls for more. If no delegation mechanism exists,
the supervisor may run the unchanged brief directly.

Give least privilege and only the required repository scope, tools,
permissions, secrets, and mutation authority. Only Worker may mutate product
state, using an exact subset of the active human-authorized implementation
boundary. Other roles
are read-only except explicitly authorized disposable verification artifacts.
Keep secret values out of prompts, reports, artifacts, and unnecessary agents.

Supervise by exception: material blocker, scope or truth conflict, safety or
authority concern, required supervisor judgment, or evidence that should stop
other work. Resume the same delegate for local correction while its brief
remains valid; use a fresh path when the problem or context changed materially.
Meaning-changing evidence stops affected mutation and returns to its causal
owner.

When several delegates are intended as independent paths or disagree, load
shared [evaluation guidance](../../references/evaluation.md). Treat each
return as evidence, inspect its target and proof, preserve disagreement and
correlation, and avoid vote-counting or confidence scores.

Return `delegated`, `partial`, `blocked`, `stopped`, or `no-op` with role,
bounded result, exact evidence and pointers, material changes, verification,
limits, deviations, and recommended next step. Keep logs and coordination
transient unless a named consumer needs them. Delegation never authorizes
correction, gates, commit, publication, deployment, or continuation.
