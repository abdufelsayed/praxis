# Reference Interpretation

Load this reference when an existing implementation, product, document,
image, behavior, or other rich source carries more useful meaning than a
verbal description. Discover Requirements keeps the inquiry, synthesis,
accepted meaning, and stopping decision.

## Inspect the highest-fidelity source

Prefer the form closest to the claim:

- working source for structure and semantics;
- observable behavior for interaction and failure;
- authoritative documentation for a declared contract;
- an image or recording for visual or temporal qualities; and
- a paper, standard, or report for its supported claim.

Record the exact path, URL, version, revision, environment, or observed state
needed to interpret the source. Make volatile conditions visible.

## Separate meaning from accident

Identify the exact behavior or quality the requester values or rejects, the
mechanism and conditions that produce it, the transferable intent, incidental
language/framework/styling/history, incompatible assumptions, and the
requirement or later judgment the source may inform. A source's implementation
shape is not automatically its meaning.

Classify authority for the claim at hand:

- **authoritative** when it defines the governing contract;
- **evidentiary** when it supports a factual conclusion;
- **exemplary** when it demonstrates one successful realization; or
- **inspirational** when it expands the possibility space.

Authority is claim-specific. Official documentation may define an API while
remaining irrelevant to a preferred interaction; an admired implementation
may be exemplary without becoming a requirement.

## Preserve interpretation and fidelity

Record what the requester wants to preserve, reject, combine, or understand. Keep
that judgment separate from what the source itself establishes. When the
requester reacts to a partial or simulated representation, preserve the fidelity
boundary: approval of a screenshot's density does not prove interaction
behavior it cannot demonstrate.

Promote only supported meaning. If the desired property depends on an
unverified mechanism or incompatible condition, recommend Research, Make
Tangible, or Interview rather than generalizing silently.

## Return the narrow interpretation

Return the exact source and conditions, authority for the current claim, valued
or rejected qualities, mechanism and transferable intent, incidental or
incompatible details, human interpretation, fidelity limits, the later judgment
informed, and unresolved questions. Discover Requirements decides what becomes
accepted truth and what remains evidence, reaction, or uncertainty.
