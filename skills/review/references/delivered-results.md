# Reviewing Delivered Results

## Claimed behavior and evidence

Trace every promised behavior and required proof in the Result to current
evidence. Check that evidence reaches the governing boundary, including
material failure paths and inherited behavior exposed by the change, and that
freshness and environment support the claim. Distinguish:

- **Failed behavior:** evidence shows the working form violates the intended
  outcome.
- **Missing or weak evidence:** behavior may work, but the evidence cannot
  support the claim.
- **Incomplete disclosure:** material limitations, deviations, learning, or
  unresolved questions are absent or understated.

Keep these mechanisms distinct; a proof gap is not the same as failed
behavior, and a disclosure gap is not the same as either.

## Learning and implementation assurance

Represent material discoveries accurately when they change governing meaning,
proof, risk, or a control boundary. A declared limitation cannot relabel an
unimplemented required behavior as deliberate. When implementation assurance
is absent, inspect the underlying working form through both Specification
Fidelity and Engineering Integrity.
