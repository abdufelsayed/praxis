# Spec Template

Use when desired behavior is stable enough to guide implementation or review. Omit empty sections.

```markdown
# <Feature Or Behavior> Spec

Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Status: draft | accepted | superseded

## Goal

<The outcome users, callers, or maintainers need.>

## Non-Goals

- <boundary that should not be solved here>

## Users Or Callers

- <person, role, service, package, API, or workflow>

## Required Behavior

- REQ-1: <observable behavior or contract>

## Constraints

- <technical, product, safety, compatibility, or workflow constraint>

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: <checkable result>

## Risks Or Unknowns

- <risk, unknown, or needed exploration>

## Links

- <related context/decision/issue/plan/evidence/source>
```
