# External evidence

Use this method when a bounded claim depends on current documentation,
standards, laws, papers, datasets, release records, or other evidence outside
the repository. Research retains the claim, synthesis, return, authority, and
stopping.

## Choose evidence close to the claim

State the exact fact, product or jurisdiction, required freshness, conditions
that could change it, and consequence of being wrong. Prefer:

- official specifications and product documentation;
- statutes, regulations, and regulator guidance;
- original research papers and datasets;
- first-party release notes, compatibility records, and technical reports; and
- source code or maintained implementation records when they define behavior.

Use secondary sources to discover, explain, or challenge primary evidence.
Several sources repeating one assertion remain one correlated line when they
derive from the same origin.

## Preserve scope and conditions

Record the source URL or locator and relevant section together with version,
publication or update date, jurisdiction, environment, product tier, browser,
platform, or other condition that bounds the claim. Distinguish a declared
contract from observed compatibility, and a historical statement from current
behavior. Generic current documentation may not establish an installed or
configured version's behavior; pair it with repository or empirical evidence
when that condition matters.

## Resolve disagreement through conditions

When strong sources disagree, test version, configuration, scope,
terminology, environment, or authority before choosing one. Return the
conditional boundary when both observations can be true. If proportionate
investigation cannot resolve the conflict, preserve both observations and the
unresolved consequence; source count and confidence language do not break the
tie.

## Qualify negative searches

Record the sources, versions, terms, dates, and sections inspected and why
they were likely to contain the answer. Terminology may differ and important
evidence may be unpublished. Absence of a result establishes only what the
search could see, not universal absence.

## Return scoped external evidence

Return the bounded answer, decisive primary sources and sections, version/date/
jurisdiction/environment scope, conditional conflicts, inference, remaining
uncertainty, and the implication for the caller. Recommend a runtime probe
when written evidence cannot establish behavior in the actual environment.
