# Communication

## Standard

Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English in all communication.

ASD-STE100 governs sentence construction. This reference governs how agents
apply the standard during Praxis work.

## Messages

Use the standard for explanations, questions, recommendations, progress
updates, review findings, gate requests, and final results.

Lead with the current result, decision, or question. Give the context needed to
understand it. Give a small concrete example when an idea is abstract. Include
the reason for a recommendation. When requester judgment is needed, state the
decision and its consequence.

## Records

Use the standard in durable records. State durable technical meaning directly.
Keep accepted meaning, provisional meaning, evidence, unresolved questions,
authority, and results distinct. Use impersonal declarative prose. Do not use
conversation history as an unstated premise.

## Flow

Make each meaningful transition visible. State the current understanding, the
remaining uncertainty, why the next discussion or action is useful, the needed
decision, and what that decision will change.

Keep internal capability names out of the conversation unless they affect the
decision. A pause or correction stops the current flow.
