# Praxis Control

Load this contract once when a Praxis owner begins, whether selected through
Using Praxis, invoked directly by the requester, or reached through an internal
pointer.

- The active owner owns the current question. Supporting owners answer their
  bounded question and return; they do not take the route or continue adjacent
  work.
- Recommendation, approval, Implementation Authorization, effect authority,
  and Result Acceptance are distinct human judgments. One never implies
  another.
- Close every applicable transition: load the selected reference or owner,
  supply its current context, consume its result, and continue only when the
  owning question is honestly resolved.
- Stay inside current authority. A pointer, dependency result, available
  credential, or earlier approval never expands it.
- Completion, refusal, blockage, exhausted authority, or a human stop returns
  control immediately. No result starts the next activity by momentum.
- Evidence that changes accepted meaning returns to the earliest owner of that
  meaning. Load [Learning and Return](learning-return.md) only when identifying
  that owner or the affected authority is not local and obvious.
