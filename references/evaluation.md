# Evaluation

Evaluation keeps a judgment tied to the evidence and conditions that produced
it. A signal is useful only for the property it can actually observe.

## Signals are not interchangeable

Distinguish these signals when their consequences differ:

- **Human preference** says what a person wanted among the options and context
  they experienced.
- **Observed evidence** records what source, runtime behavior, a tool, or an
  experiment showed.
- **Critique** explains a concrete failure mechanism, consequence, or design
  concern.
- **Verification** tests an exact claim with current falsifiable evidence.
- **Approval** provisionally accepts one complete record as the current basis
  for later judgment.
- **Acceptance** judges and closes one delivered result.

One signal does not silently rewrite another. Preserve conflicts and their
consequences. Approval and acceptance are human control acts, not reward
values; a passing check does not erase a consequential review finding.

## Preference is contextual

Record a reaction as selected, rejected, mixed, tied, uncertain, or
none-of-the-above whenever that distinction changes later judgment. Keep the
reason at the precision a receiving owner needs. A mixed choice can retain one
candidate's structure while selecting another's recovery behavior; recording
only the winner loses that meaning.

Treat feedback as a local correction by default. Promote it into a durable
Requirement, repository rule, or skill rule only when a named future consumer
needs the broader rule and repeated evidence, an explicit human statement, or a
recurring mechanism supports that scope.

## Comparison needs honest contrast

Alternatives earn their cost only when contrast can reveal a consequential
preference, assumption, or trade-off. Compare at comparable fidelity and
detail, and make differences in polish, completeness, data realism, or
implementation depth visible. Label sourced fact, inference, proposal,
simulation, and placeholder content.

Interpret a reaction only at the level the presented surface can support;
equalize detail only when an imbalance could change the judgment. When an
accepted comparison or diff baseline is unavailable or unreliable, present the
complete target instead of inventing a comparison.

Make ties, mixed reactions, uncertainty, rejection, and reframing easy to
express. Preserve rationale and annotations, not only a selected option.
Ordering, verbosity, polish, framing, latency, and hidden simulation can bias
a reaction; control an obvious bias when practical, otherwise disclose it and
narrow the conclusion. Artificial alternatives are not evidence.

## Contrast must add information

Multiple candidates, probes, or delegates should pursue distinct hypotheses,
mechanisms, or evidence paths. Agreement produced from the same assumptions is
one path, not independent confirmation. If attempts are equivalent, fail for
the same reason, or cannot change the judgment, convergence, reframing, a new
evidence class, or a return to the unresolved owner is more informative than
repetition.

## Bind feedback to its context

Feedback applies to the candidate, evidence, evaluator context, and material
conditions actually judged. A change to behavior, constraints, source,
environment, model, harness, or evaluator can invalidate part or all of the
conclusion. Preserve still-valid learning, then revalidate only the affected
judgment against current state. Earlier Plans, reviews, tests, and artifacts
remain evidence; they do not become authority over a changed target.

Outcome evaluation asks whether a bounded claim holds. Process evaluation asks
whether the result was produced coherently, maintainably, and faithfully to its
specification. A successful outcome does not cancel a valid process finding,
and a sound process does not prove the outcome. Add step-level scrutiny only
where an intermediate decision could hide a meaningful failure. Verify owns
exact outcome claims; Review owns consequential failure mechanisms, design
degradation, specification drift, and honest coverage. Neither evaluator
substitutes for the other.

## Resist proxy over-optimization

Tests, review findings, coverage, Issue completion, diff size, evaluator
agreement, approval, and other signals observe limited properties. Keep the
dimensions relevant to the current judgment separate; do not collapse them
into a universal score. A hard constraint is a veto, and improvement in one
proxy cannot compensate for an authority breach, specification violation,
security failure, regression, or concrete changeability damage.

When repeated steering could overfit the evaluator, add proportionate
disconfirming evidence: a boundary-level or held-out test, different input,
existing regression, perturbed environment, independent review, runtime
observation, or human inspection. Low-risk work needs no ceremonial holdout
set.

## Independence is evidence, not a count

Reviewers that receive the same implementer summary and repeat the same
assumptions remain correlated. The synthesizing owner preserves decisive
evidence and disagreement, identifies factual versus human-owned judgment, and
recommends the smallest resolving probe or decision. Evaluator count,
confidence language, and repeatability do not substitute for independence.

## Compact feedback

An evaluator's compact feedback records only what is needed to interpret this
judgment:

```text
Feedback {
  target_or_attempt,
  signal,
  observation,
  interpretation,
  evaluator_context?,
  decisive_evidence?,
  bias_or_limitation?,
  uncertainty_or_disagreement?,
}
```

`signal` distinguishes preference, observed evidence, critique, verification,
approval, or acceptance. Keep only the provenance required by its named future
consumer. Any durable projection follows the shared record-ownership rule.
Causal owner, affected truth, still-valid truth, and any recommendation belong
to the learning return that a receiver interprets; they are not evaluator
feedback fields.

## A feedback loop stops teaching

Continue local evaluation only while another bounded attempt can plausibly
change the judgment. Convergence, reframing, a new evidence class, or a return
is appropriate when the claim is sufficiently supported, attempts add no
contrast, the evaluator cannot observe the disputed property, the result is a
human-owned judgment, governing truth is invalidated, or the next action lacks
authority. This describes the local feedback signal; the owning skill keeps its
route, broader stopping decision, and control.
